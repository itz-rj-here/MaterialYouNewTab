Step-by-Step Instructions

Walkthrough Guide

01

Visit the WeatherAPI Website

Navigate to the official WeatherAPI portal at https://www.weatherapi.com/.

https://www.weatherapi.com
Click to enlarge
02

Sign Up for an Account

  1. Click on the Sign Up button located at the top right corner of the homepage.
  2. Fill in the required fields:
    • Email Address: Provide a valid email address that you have access to.
    • Password: Create a strong, secure password.
  3. Agree to the terms and conditions.
  4. Complete the CAPTCHA verification if prompted.
  5. Click Sign Up to create your free developer account.
Already registered? If you already have a WeatherAPI account, simply click Login and proceed with your existing credentials.
weatherapi.com/signup.aspx
Click to enlarge
03

Verify Your Email Address

Check your email inbox for a verification email sent by WeatherAPI. Open the message and click on the confirmation link to activate your account.

Helpful Tip: If the email doesn't arrive within 1–2 minutes, be sure to check your spam, junk, or promotions folder.
04

Log In to Your Account

  1. Return to the WeatherAPI website.
  2. Click on Login in the top navigation bar.
  3. Enter your registered email address and password.
  4. Click Login to access your dashboard.
weatherapi.com/login.aspx
Click to enlarge
05

Copy Your API Key

  1. Once logged in, open the Dashboard.
  2. Locate the API Key section on your account overview.
  3. You will see your private API key string displayed alongside a convenient Copy icon. Click it to copy your key to your clipboard.
weatherapi.com/my/
Click to enlarge
06

Paste Your API Key into MYNT

  1. Open a new tab or open your Material You New Tab extension settings.
  2. Under the Weather options, paste your API key into the WeatherAPI key input field.
  3. Press Enter or click the Save button to activate it.
Success! Your weather widget is now powered by your dedicated key, ensuring lightning-fast responses, accurate forecasts, and location auto-suggest.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

By default, the extension estimates your geographic location using your IP address, which is often routed through your internet service provider's regional hub or proxy server. If this differs from your real location:

  • Manually type your exact city name or geographic coordinates (lat, lon) into the settings.
  • Switch on the GPS / Geolocation option in the extension to fetch hardware-level precision.

An API key serves as a unique credential for managing access and monitoring weather requests:

  • Shared Quota Exhaustion: Material You New Tab provides built-in shared keys that allow up to 15,000,000 monthly calls. With over 300,000+ active users worldwide, this collective quota can occasionally be exhausted near month-end. When exhausted, weather updates pause until the monthly reset.
  • Your Dedicated Allocation: Adding your own personal key gives you an independent allowance of 1,000,000 calls per month exclusively for your browser, guaranteeing zero downtime.

Registering your personal key brings significant quality-of-life benefits:

  • Zero Disruption: Complete immunity from community usage caps. Your forecast will always display smoothly.
  • Location Autocomplete: Unlocks live search recommendations as you type in the location field for quick, accurate selection.
  • Usage Analytics: Monitor your personal request statistics, bandwidth, and latency logs directly in your WeatherAPI dashboard.
  • Privacy & Security: Your queries are linked solely to your personal developer account, ensuring full control.
  • Full Compliance: Complete compliance with WeatherAPI's fair use policies.
